# Commit messages and PR descriptions

Apply this guide on top of `SKILL.md`.

- Write the subject in the imperative, as `<type>(<scope>): <subject>`.
- In the body, say what the change does, then why, then what it supersedes or breaks.
- Describe the resulting state only when it carries a design choice that the reviewer should notice.
- Give the reader's next step: what to apply first, what to re-run, what now fails differently.
